# Bird Adventure Game

## Project Overview

Welcome to Bird Adventure Game! This is an exciting bird-themed side-scrolling game where you guide your feathered friends through challenging levels. The game is developed using the Unity engine.

## Core Gameplay Mechanics

The main goal in Bird Adventure Game is to control a bird, skillfully navigating through a series of dynamic levels. Here are some of the key gameplay elements:

*   **Flight and Obstacle Avoidance:** Guide your bird to fly through the levels while avoiding various obstacles such as Fire and FireWalls.
*   **Collectibles:** Gather items like Food scattered throughout the levels to aid your bird.
*   **HP Management:** Keep an eye on your bird's health (HP) as you encounter challenges.
*   **Strategic FireWalls:** Players have the ability to strategically place FireWalls to overcome certain obstacles or protect their bird.
*   **Level Progression:** Advance through multiple levels, each potentially offering new challenges and environments.
*   **Win/Lose Conditions:** The game features clear win and game over states based on your performance.

## Out-Game Features

Beyond the main gameplay, Bird Adventure Game offers several features to enhance your experience:

*   **Team Building:** Assemble and manage your own team of unique birds.
*   **Gacha System:** Discover and acquire new bird species through an engaging gacha mechanic.
*   **Bird Collection:** View detailed information and artwork for all the birds you have collected.
*   **Menu Navigation:** Easy-to-use menus for accessing different parts of the game like team management, gacha, and starting new game sessions.

## How to Play (Basics)

Getting started with Bird Adventure Game is simple:

*   **Navigate Menus:** Use the on-screen buttons to navigate through the main menu, team management, and other sections.
*   **In-Game Controls:**
    *   Control your bird's flight (details may vary depending on the bird or level).
    *   Strategically place items like FireWalls when prompted or able.
*   **Objective:** Your primary goal is to guide your bird safely through each level, collect food, and reach the end. Experiment with different birds and strategies to master the game!

*(Specific controls and advanced tactics will become clearer as you play and explore the different features of the game.)*

## Technical Details

*   **Engine:** Developed using the Unity game engine.
*   **Data Management:** Utilizes ScriptableObjects for flexible and organized game data configuration (e.g., bird stats, game settings).
*   **Design Patterns:** Implements common software design patterns such as the Singleton pattern for managing global game states.
